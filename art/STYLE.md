# Warden of Power: style guide

Approved art direction: October 20, 2026 (ART_DIRECTION.md). This file gathers it in one place for every new brief (game-art-briefs skill). Changes to anything here need the user's approval.

## Style sentence (start every art prompt with it)
Painted cartoon fantasy, three-quarter top-down camera, chunky readable shapes with dark coloured outlines, rich elemental colours, warm golden light from the upper left, clean flat play areas with detail kept to the borders.

## Reference board (art/reference)
- towers-and-enemies-approved.png: the canonical tower, enemy and projectile look (approved Oct 20).
- map-arena-example-emberheart.jpg and map-arena-example-moonwell.jpg: the battle-map standard (walled arena, elemental border, flat build floor, glowing seal).
- game/assets/hub/hub-restored.jpg: the home base.
- game/assets/badges/*-256.png: the badge frame family.

## Camera and light
- Camera: three-quarter top-down, about 35 degrees; buildings show their front face and roof.
- Light: warm, from the upper left; soft shadows to the lower right.
- Outlines: dark, coloured (not black) outlines on every object; maps keep outlines on borders and props, not on the floor.

Each sector's map, towers, hits and UI chips use its element colour.

## Fonts
- Display: Lilita One (OFL), shown in the game as the family "Warden Quill". Titles, numbers, banners, covers.
- Body: Nunito variable (OFL), family "Warden Text". Everything else.
- Minimum rendered size: 10 px at the smallest window (821x462); test line "Warden of Power · WAVE 12 · 1.04K · Fire, Water, Ice".

## Visible growth
Each tower has Tier I (approved base), Tier II and two Tier III specialisations; every step must look clearly bigger or more ornate at play size.

## First map
- The first map a new player sees: the Outer Seal ("First Watch", game/assets/maps/outer-seal.jpg), painted Oct 2026.
- Status Oct 28 2026: a repaint to the arena standard is approved (art/prompts/outer-seal_v2.md). Record the date here when the new painting is in.

## Audio (one sentence)
Warm fantasy: soft bells, plucked and bowed tones, gentle percussion, no harsh synths; effects and music around -22 to -26 LUFS, ambience quieter.
