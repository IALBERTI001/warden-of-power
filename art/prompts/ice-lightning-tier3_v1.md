# Ice and Lightning Tier III repaint: brief and prompts (v1, Oct 28 2026)

Approved by the user on Oct 28 2026 (rules check item 6). At play size the Ice and Lightning Tier II and both Tier III looks are too alike; every upgrade must change the tower's look at a glance. Fire's three looks are the standard to match (Tier II adds twin chimneys, Fracture furnace adds a big cannon mouth).

## Brief
- **Purpose:** the two Tier III specialisations of the Ice and Lightning towers. Each must be recognisable at about 75 px tall on the battlefield.
- **Keep:** the approved Tier I base and Tier II (unchanged), the same stone foundation, camera, size, palette and dark outlines as the approved towers.
- **Ice, Cold front** (splash chill, stronger slow): a wider tower with a ring of drifting frost mist and three tall crystal spires, plus a snowflake banner on each side.
- **Ice, Crystal focus** (higher damage, longer range): one tall central prism held in a gold focusing ring, with a bright pale-blue beam point at its tip.
- **Lightning, Chain reaction** (arcs through up to five targets): three conductor rods with glowing orbs and arcs jumping between them.
- **Lightning, Focused current** (concentrated damage): one large violet crystal inside a tight copper coil, with a gold lightning-rod crown.
- **Format:** both looks of one element in one image, side by side in two equal-width columns with a clear gap, each tower centred with the whole tower in frame and about 8% margin, on a solid flat magenta #FF00FF background with no shadow on the background.

## Attach these images (from the Online Games\art folder)
1. `art\reference\towers-and-enemies-approved.png` (the approved tower style)
2. `art\reference\ice-tiers-current.png` or `art\reference\lightning-tiers-current.png` (current Tier II and Tier III: keep the base and Tier II, redesign the two Tier III towers)
