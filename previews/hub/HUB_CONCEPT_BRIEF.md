## What the hub is
The player's home screen: an overview of the whole ancient elemental Containment Complex, seen from the same high three-quarter camera as the tutorial map, as one illustrated game map. It must read clearly at a glance: a few big, distinct landmarks, clear paths between them, calm open space for interface panels on the right third.

Layout (landscape 3:2, 1536x1024):
- CENTRE: the **Aetherheart Engine** — a great round brass-and-stone engine hall, sealed shut with dark runes, holding a dormant swirling portal (the Unbound Gate) inside a ring of pillars.
- SOUTH (bottom centre): the **Outer Seal** — the arched stone gatehouse from the tutorial map, already restored, with a lit lantern.
- NORTH-WEST: **Moonwell Cisterns** (water) — tiered stone pools and aqueducts with a moon-shaped well.
- NORTH-EAST: **Emberheart Forge** (fire) — a stone forge hall with tall chimneys and a great furnace mouth.
- SOUTH-WEST: **Rimeglass Vaults** (ice) — a half-buried vault with crystalline ice doors.
- EAST: **Stormcrown Spire** (lightning) — a tall spire crowned with copper coils.
- SOUTH-EAST near the Outer Seal: **Cogwright's Atelier** (engineering) — a small workshop with gears and a crane.
- Worn stone paths connect every landmark to the Aetherheart Engine. Overgrown grass, moss, vines and rubble between them.
- No text, labels, letters, UI, characters or towers. No watermark.
