# One scene, one art direction

The user rejected the separate road/door/background construction as visually mismatched. This revision supersedes those scene-compositing proposals; do not treat earlier portal or procedural-road attempts as approved art.

`game/assets/broken-conduit-integrated-candidate.png` is a single painted courtyard containing the road, masonry and both dark tunnel entrances, generated with the built-in image tool. Prompt specified an orthographic three-quarter limestone/copper containment complex, subdued surrounding ground, broad worn serpentine paving and two wall-integrated open arches. Actual route points were traced from the generated image rather than assuming the generator obeyed requested coordinates exactly.

The game no longer draws the procedural road or the isolated arch sprite. The ground/view transforms are now identity because this integrated scene already contains its camera projection. Upright approved tower/enemy sprites retain their existing artwork. Foreground masonry is re-rendered from clipped portions of the SAME scene texture at the SAME coordinates; no different portal artwork is pasted over it. Tunnel reveal masks and target exposure follow those openings.

New candidate map must still receive visual approval. Engine and mocked UI/projection checks cannot establish artistic quality. The existing file-URL browser tool restriction still prevents the agent's live visual inspection.

Saved badges/resources remain. Existing towers that conflict with the rebuilt route or dock boundary are refunded at their full invested Charge, once when migrating from the previous scene. Valid towers are retained. A toast communicates any refund; no permanent account progression is removed. New saves include sceneVersion 2. The changed map requires fresh playtesting of coverage and pacing.
