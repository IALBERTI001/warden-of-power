# Approved endgame plan

Approved October 21, 2026.

## Core philosophy

**Players are the life-blood of our games. Our focus is the player and their experience.**

Players should have something rewarding to earn now, something exciting to reach next, and someone to overtake. Progression and the competitive ladder motivate returning; strategy supports enjoyment and replayability.

## Entry

Campaign clears and required facility restorations unlock the central engine and endless mode. Introduce Resonance and grant enough for a meaningful first skill purchase. Reveal the endgame in understandable steps, beginning with a clear first wave milestone.

## Permanent progression

Five branches: fire, water, ice, lightning, engineering. Foundation offers affordable improvements; specialization strengthens favorite towers and combos; mastery opens further power tiers after the initial tree. Players may eventually purchase every foundational upgrade. Free reassignment occurs between attempts only. Endless bonuses do not change campaign balance. Mastery uses bounded, expandable tiers, not an untested promise of infinitely repeatable upgrades.

## Wave milestones

Deeper waves introduce enemy formations, combinations of roles and recognizable bosses, not only larger health values. Milestones offer bonus Resonance, achievements/badges, mastery-tier access and cosmetic recognition. Show the next milestone and its reward before a run. Specific intervals, earnings and prices require playtesting.

## Ladder

Account-based all-time Top 10 remains prominent. Also show personal best, own rank, nearby competitors and the next completed-wave record to beat. Account power growth is an intended part of competition. Validate submissions and keep records comparable across major balance versions. Local development uses labeled test data until online integration exists.

## Results

Show highest completed wave, personal-best result, Resonance earned and total, new achievements/milestones, ladder change, and a player-selected upgrade goal with remaining cost. Offer clear actions to upgrade or retry. Defeat preserves rewards already banked.

## Development sequence

First prove the foundation tree, a meaningful stretch of endless waves, milestone rewards and personal-best tracking. Tune how progress feels through playtests before extending mastery and connecting competitive services. The 20-node skill-tree mockup remains a presentation proposal with illustrative values; approving this loop does not approve every displayed number or final icon.

## Current freeze contract

Freeze requires soaked and consumes it. Freeze lasts 1.5 seconds, cannot be refreshed by additional hits, and is followed by 4 seconds of resistance on natural thaw or 2 seconds on fire shatter. Existing visual effect and movement/death treatments are approved. Bosses use shorter freezes with exact values still to be specified.
