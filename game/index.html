Elements & combinations

Fire: direct damage + 3 seconds of burning.

Water: light damage + Soaked (4 seconds at Tier I). Soaked alone does not slow.

Ice: Chilled slows by 24% for 2.7 seconds at Tier I.

Soaked + Ice: consumes Soaked to freeze for 1.5 seconds. Natural thaw gives 4 seconds of freeze resistance.

Soaked + Lightning: conducts through up to 3 nearby enemies at Tier I.

Frozen + Fire: shatters for area damage; freeze resistance is only 2 seconds.
